% The absurdity of asking what they learned


I am being hit by the absurdity of the question "what did you
learn?". I could summarize what I learned but it wouldn't teach you
what I learned, only some of it. Contrast this with discovery—where
the work I did to learn a new thing can now be bypassed.



_Martin Jambon, January 12, 2026 \[[explainer](/notes/what-did-you-discover/explainer/)\]_
